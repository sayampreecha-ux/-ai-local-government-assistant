# GP002 — Government Law Copilot

## Architecture

GP002 is a new module layered exclusively on the public Sprint 1 Core APIs. It does
not alter Core or GP001. Construction idempotently registers prompt `2.0.0`, eleven
controlled knowledge-source descriptors, RBAC policies, the workflow definition,
six analysis templates, and a PDPA approval rule.

The workflow is deterministic: input validation, routing, PDPA detection, knowledge
retrieval and authorization, authority analysis, citation ranking and validation,
conflict detection, timeline classification, legal reasoning, risk scoring, prompt
authorization, approval gating, output validation, and correlated audit/metrics.

## Engines

- Authority Engine ranks legal hierarchy and identifies competent agency,
  delegation evidence, and jurisdiction.
- Reasoning Engine returns facts, issues, applicable law, analysis, conclusion,
  recommendation, and a bounded confidence score.
- Citation Engine validates and ranks multiple references and produces cross-references.
- Conflict Engine compares versions, effective dates, and higher/lower hierarchy.
- Timeline Engine classifies effective, repealed, superseded, and pending sources.
- Risk Engine emits high, medium, or low risk with a deterministic uncertainty score.
- PDPA Engine detects identifiers without retaining raw findings and recursively masks output.
- Output Engine supports Markdown, JSON, audit-log, and API-response envelopes.

## Registered knowledge

Acts, Royal Decrees, Ministerial Regulations, Ministry of Interior Regulations,
Treasury Regulations, Local Government Regulations, DLA Circulars, Council
Resolutions, Legal Opinions, Court Decisions, and Audit Findings are registered as
controlled source references. The `govprompt://` entries are metadata pointers, not
embedded legal text; production adapters must resolve them to authoritative corpora.

## Templates

Legal Opinion, Legal Analysis, Authority Analysis, Consultation Draft, Executive
Summary, and One Page Legal Brief use the same validated analysis model, ensuring
that changing presentation never changes the underlying legal result.

## Safety and limits

GP002 is deny-by-default. Only authorized legal roles may read registered knowledge
or execute the prompt. Personal data triggers a Governance approval gate and an
audit event before prompt resolution. Every conclusion remains provisional and
instructs the operator to verify cited text against the controlled official source.

## Demo

Run `node demo/gp002-demo.js`. It creates an isolated Core runtime, completes a legal
opinion in API-response format, and prints the response and diagnostic summary.
