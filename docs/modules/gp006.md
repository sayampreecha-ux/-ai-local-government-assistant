# GP006 — HR Copilot

GP006 is a standalone deterministic HR module built on the six Sprint 1 Core
services. It injects GP002 for legal review, GP004 for financial eligibility, and
GP005 for budget validation without changing existing modules. Startup idempotently
registers prompt `2.0.0`, a 21-stage workflow, six templates, ten controlled
personnel knowledge references, and deny-by-default HR policies.

Its engines cover appointment authority and vacancy, promotion tenure/performance,
transfer consent and jurisdiction, salary increments and caps, position allowances,
disciplinary evidence and due process, retirement age, workforce projections, and
position qualification standards. A consolidated HR engine produces a confidence,
decision, gaps, recommendation, and linked-module evidence.

Templates include HR Opinion, Promotion Analysis, Transfer Analysis, Disciplinary
Report, Executive Summary, and One Page. JSON, Markdown, audit-log, and API-response
formats share the same validated analysis. Knowledge pointers cover the local
personnel act, position standards, ก.จ./ก.ท./ก.อบต. announcements, DLA letters,
consultation replies, rulings, OCSC practice, and related regulations. Production
adapters must resolve `govprompt://` references to current official sources.

Run `node demo/gp006-demo.js` for an executable promotion analysis and diagnostics.
