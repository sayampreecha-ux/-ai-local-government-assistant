# GP008 — Project Copilot

GP008 is a standalone deterministic project module over the six Sprint 1 Core
services. GP002 legal, GP003 procurement, GP004 finance, GP005 budget, and GP007
council copilots are injected through their stable `execute()` interface. Existing
modules are unchanged. Startup idempotently registers prompt `2.0.0`, a 23-stage
workflow, six templates, eight knowledge references, and project RBAC policies.

The engines assess proposal structure and beneficiaries, five feasibility
dimensions, mandate and competent authority, project/plan/available budget, KPI
quality, milestone dates and dependencies, procurement readiness, risk exposure,
six monitoring controls, and a recommend/revise decision. Outputs are deterministic
Markdown, JSON, audit-log, or API-response envelopes.

Templates are วิเคราะห์โครงการ, One Page, Executive Summary, Checklist, Risk Matrix,
and KPI Report. Knowledge covers Ministry of Interior regulation, DLA directives,
budget and procurement rules, local development and budget plans, SAO audit guidance,
and NACC guidance. Production adapters must resolve `govprompt://` pointers to
current official sources.

Run `node demo/gp008-demo.js` for an executable project decision and diagnostics.
