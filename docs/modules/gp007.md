# GP007 — Council Copilot

GP007 is a standalone deterministic council-procedure module built on the six Sprint
1 Core services. It injects GP002 legal, GP005 budget, and GP006 HR copilots for
explicit linked reviews without modifying existing modules. Startup idempotently
registers prompt `2.0.0`, a 21-stage workflow, six templates, eight controlled
council knowledge references, and deny-by-default council policies.

The pipeline validates notice and agenda documents, motion form and scope, strict
majority quorum, simple/absolute/two-thirds voting, resolution validity, ordinance
readings and consultation, competent authority and delegation, meeting minutes and
certification, nine compliance controls, and council risk. Outputs are deterministic
Markdown, JSON, audit-log, or API-response envelopes.

Templates cover Motion Review, Council Legal Opinion, Agenda Review, Meeting
Checklist, Executive Summary, and One Page. Knowledge covers the PAO, Municipality,
and Tambon/SAO Acts, Ministry of Interior council-meeting regulation, DLA directives,
rulings and consultations, SAO audit guidance, and NACC guidance. Production adapters
must resolve `govprompt://` pointers to current authoritative sources.

Run `node demo/gp007-demo.js` for an executable municipal resolution review and
diagnostic output.
