# GP004 — Finance Copilot

## Architecture

GP004 is a new module layered on the six Sprint 1 Core services. It receives GP002
Law Copilot and GP003 Procurement Copilot as injected dependencies, allowing an
explicit finance request to include legal and procurement review inputs without
coupling their internal engines. Existing modules and Core are unchanged.

Startup idempotently registers prompt `2.0.0`, the workflow definition, six output
templates, seven controlled finance knowledge references, and role-based policies.
After cross-module calls, GP004 restores itself as the active ContextManager module.

## Deterministic workflow

The pipeline validates inputs, routes to GP004, authorizes knowledge, optionally runs
GP002 and GP003 reviews, checks eligibility and budget availability, analyzes travel,
allowances, reimbursements and training expenses, assesses financial risk, builds a
nine-item audit checklist, evaluates compliance, authorizes and resolves the prompt,
validates output, updates budget context, and emits correlated audits and metrics.

## Coverage

- Official travel transport, lodging, subsistence, and meal caps
- Reimbursement documentation and cap checks
- Training cost-per-participant and approval checks
- House rental, medical, welfare, and local maintenance-fund allowances
- Budget commitment, availability, utilization, and remaining balance
- Financial risk and payment-readiness audit checklist

## Knowledge and templates

Controlled references cover Ministry of Interior and Ministry of Finance regulations,
Comptroller General circulars, DLA letters, consultation replies, finance rulings, and
State Audit Office guidance. Production adapters must resolve `govprompt://` pointers
to current authoritative sources.

Templates are Finance Opinion, Reimbursement Analysis, Travel Analysis, Financial
Checklist, Executive Summary, and One Page Finance Brief. JSON, Markdown, audit-log,
and API-response envelopes share the same validated analysis object.

## Demo

Run `node demo/gp004-demo.js`. The demo initializes Core plus GP002 and GP003,
performs a deterministic reimbursement analysis, and prints its API response and
diagnostics.
